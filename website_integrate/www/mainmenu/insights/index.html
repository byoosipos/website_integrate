Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about cold storage solutions

How can businesses reduce energy costs in cold storage?

Investing in energy-efficient refrigeration system (Inverter Units), solar-powered refrigeration, and IoT-enabled monitoring can help reduce costs.

What industries benefit most from cold storage solutions?

Agriculture, pharmaceuticals, retail outlets, logistics, hospitality, oil and gas, food and beverages and aviation benefit significantly from reliable cold storage.

How does De Koolar ensure compliance with industry regulations?

We design, manufacture and install cold storage systems that meet national and international regulatory standards, ensuring optimal temperature control.

What are the key factors to consider when investing in a cold storage facility?

Location, government policy, energy available and efficiency, scalability, market, source of reliable product, quality after-sales services and compliance with industry regulations are crucial for a successful investment.
