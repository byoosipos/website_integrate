Investment Opportunities in Cold Storage

With increasing demand for efficient cold storage solutions, businesses can explore various investment opportunities in Nigeria's growing cold chain sector.

Industry Statistics

Available records show that 45% of products such as tomatoes, pepper and onions in Nigeria end as post-harvest losses, highlighting the urgent need for private investors in the cold storage sector.

Large-Scale Cold Storage Facilities

Agricultural, pharmaceuticals, retail sectors and other industries can leverage these facilities for their storage needs. Opportunity for facility leasing and management.

Cold Chain Logistics

Investing in refrigerated transportation and storage networks to create an integrated cold chain solution for businesses.

Renewable Energy Solutions

Leveraging solar and hybrid energy solutions for cost-efficient cold storage operations, reducing operational expenses while being environmentally conscious.

Blast Facilities

Investors can off-take from poultry farmers, blast freeze and sell to retailers. Farm owners can also pay certain fees to access the facility.

Cold Storage Warehousing

Crucial investment opportunity in developing countries in Africa where there is gross deficit of cold storage facilities for storage of local farm produce.
