Industry Challenges and Solutions

High Energy Costs

Cold storage facilities consume significant energy. This is where De Koolar Nigeria Limited comes in by implementing energy-efficient designs, leveraging on renewable energy sources to mitigate high costs.

Regulatory Compliance

Strict industry regulations require businesses to maintain specific temperature ranges. De Koolar provides solutions that ensure compliance with national and international standards.

Supply Chain Disruptions

Ensuring an unbroken cold chain is essential for perishable goods. Investing in backup power solutions and real-time monitoring systems enhances reliability.
