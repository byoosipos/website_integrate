A Message from Our CEO

March 20, 2024 | By Ola Akanbi

Welcome to De Koolar Nigeria Limited. It is with great pride and immense pleasure that I introduce you to a company where innovation, trust, and excellence are the core principles that drive everything we do. As the CEO of this remarkable organization, I am honored to lead a team that has grown from humble beginnings to establish itself as a prominent and respected leader in the HVAC/R industry. With more than two decades of experience, De Koolar has consistently delivered cutting-edge solutions, revolutionizing the cold value chain and shaping the future of heat transfer systems.

At De Koolar, our journey has been defined by a deep commitment to innovation and a desire to meet the diverse and ever-evolving needs of our clients. Our goal is simple: to provide energy-efficient, sustainable, and reliable HVAC/R solutions that are tailored to the specific needs of each customer. While the manufacturing of high-quality products is an integral part of our business, it is our focus on enhancing operational efficiency through advanced systems—such as cold/freezer rooms, blast freezers, and sustainable energy solutions—that truly differentiates us in the marketplace. By reducing operational costs and integrating the latest technologies, we help businesses stay ahead of the curve and future-proof their operations.

One of the key factors behind our success lies in the strategic relationships we have built with globally renowned Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs). These partnerships ensure that the products and services we offer consistently meet the highest international standards. This collaborative approach allows us to stay at the forefront of industry developments, ensuring that our solutions are always aligned with the latest technological advancements.

The core values that define De Koolar—innovation, excellence, customer satisfaction, integrity, passion, and sustainability—are more than just words; they are the principles that guide every decision we make. From designing and manufacturing world-class products to delivering exceptional installation, maintenance, and repair services, our focus remains on our customers. We prioritize a customer-centric approach, ensuring that each solution we provide is not only innovative but also customized to meet the unique needs of every client, guaranteeing satisfaction at every step.

Our company's reputation as a pioneer in cold storage infrastructure speaks volumes about our commitment to quality, reliability, and efficiency. We take immense pride in our ability to stay ahead of the technological curve, continuously embracing advancements that enable us to provide the highest level of service. At De Koolar, we don't just adapt to industry trends—we lead the way.

Looking ahead, we are committed to continuing our tradition of delivering forward-thinking heat transfer solutions that address today's challenges while shaping the future of the HVAC/R industry. Our team—composed of skilled engineers, dedicated technicians, and exceptional support staff—is our most valuable asset. Together, we remain focused on ensuring that every product we develop and every service we offer sets new benchmarks for performance, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Thank you for choosing De Koolar Nigeria Limited. By partnering with us, you are not just selecting advanced, energy-efficient solutions; you are becoming part of a legacy built on integrity, trust, and industry leadership. We look forward to the opportunity to work alongside you, collaborating to achieve extraordinary results and continue our journey of excellence.

Sincerely,

Engr. Ola Akanbi

Chief Executive Officer
De Koolar Nigeria Limited
