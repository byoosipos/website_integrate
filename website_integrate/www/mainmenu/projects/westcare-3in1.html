Project Completed Westcare Foods

3-in-1 Cold Storage Facility

Multi-zone Storage Solution in Idimu, Lagos State

Project Overview

A state-of-the-art 3-in-1 cold storage facility has been successfully installed at Idimu, Lagos State, sponsored by Westcare Foods. This versatile installation features three distinct temperature zones, each optimized for specific storage requirements, from bottled water to frozen foods.

Bottled Water Zone

Dedicated chiller room for optimal water storage conditions.

Temperature +/-5°C
Capacity 4 Tonnes
Power 3 HP Unit

Perishables Zone

Temperature-controlled environment for fresh food preservation.

Temperature +/-5°C
Capacity 15 Tonnes
Power 5 HP Unit

Frozen Foods Zone

Deep freeze storage for long-term preservation.

Temperature -18°C
Capacity 20 Tonnes
Power 7.5 HP Unit

Technical Specifications

Construction

  • 100mm Polyurethane Sandwich Panels
  • 42kg/m³ Panel Density (±3kg/m³)
  • PPGI Sheet (0.8mm thickness)
  • Cam-lock Panel Grip System
  • Polystyrene Floor Insulation

Power Systems

  • 380-415V Three Phase Supply
  • 8kVA - 25kVA Generator Support
  • 2.5mm - 6mm Cable Sizing
  • 50Hz Operating Frequency
  • Phase Monitoring Protection

Refrigeration

  • Danfross, Copeland & Bitzer Units
  • R22 Refrigerant System
  • Split Unit Configuration
  • Thermostatic Expansion
  • Digital Temperature Control
