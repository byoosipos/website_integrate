Westcare Hospital Project

Advanced medical cold chain solutions for healthcare excellence

Project Overview

DeKoolar partnered with Westcare Hospital to implement a comprehensive medical cold chain solution, ensuring the safe storage of vital medical supplies, vaccines, and laboratory specimens.

Our integrated approach combines cutting-edge refrigeration technology with smart monitoring systems, maintaining precise temperature control for critical healthcare materials.

Implementation Phases

Phase 1: Assessment & Planning

Comprehensive evaluation of storage requirements and facility infrastructure to design optimal cold chain solutions.

Phase 2: Infrastructure Setup

Installation of medical-grade cold storage units and backup power systems for uninterrupted operation.

Phase 3: Monitoring Integration

Implementation of advanced temperature monitoring and alert systems for 24/7 surveillance.

Phase 4: Staff Training

Comprehensive training programs for hospital staff on system operation and maintenance protocols.

Implemented Solutions

Temperature Control

Precision temperature management systems for different medical storage requirements.

Backup Systems

Redundant power solutions ensuring continuous operation during power outages.

Remote Monitoring

Real-time temperature monitoring and automated alert systems.
