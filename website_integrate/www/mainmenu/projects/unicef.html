UNICEF Cold Chain Project

Strengthening vaccine cold chain infrastructure across Nigeria through innovative refrigeration solutions

Project Overview

In partnership with UNICEF, DeKoolar has implemented a comprehensive cold chain solution to support Nigeria's immunization program. This project focuses on providing reliable vaccine storage and transportation systems across multiple states.

Our cold chain solutions ensure vaccine potency is maintained throughout the supply chain, from central storage to the last mile delivery points, helping to protect millions of children across Nigeria.
