Allamanda Cold Room Project

Project Overview

DeKoolar collaborated with Allamanda to design and implement a comprehensive cold storage solution that meets the demanding requirements of a luxury hospitality establishment.

Our custom-designed cold rooms ensure optimal preservation of fresh produce, meats, and perishables while maximizing energy efficiency and operational convenience.

Temperature Control

Multi-zone temperature management with precise digital controls for different food categories.

Easy Access

Wide doorways and organized storage systems for efficient loading and retrieval.

Food Safety

HACCP compliant systems with advanced monitoring and recording features.

Energy Efficient

Smart power management and high-efficiency cooling systems.

Remote Monitoring

24/7 temperature monitoring with mobile alerts and reporting.

Air Circulation

Advanced airflow design for uniform temperature distribution.
