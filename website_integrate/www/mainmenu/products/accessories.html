Refrigeration & Cooling Accessories

De Koolar's accessories are components that improve the performance of your all our system be it a cold-room, blast freezer-room, chiller-room, tunnel blast and more.

They are essential part of our machines that can help to prolong the life of the system. Our accessories serve multiple critical functions:

  • Safety devices for system protection
  • Performance enhancement components
  • System control and monitoring tools
  • Regulation and optimization devices

All our equipment and accessories come from well-known OEMs/Brands globally.
