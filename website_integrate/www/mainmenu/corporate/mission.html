Mission & Vision

Our purpose and aspirations driving us forward

Our Mission

To provide innovative and sustainable refrigeration solutions that exceed customer expectations while contributing to Nigeria's industrial growth through excellence in service, technology, and environmental responsibility.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of refrigeration solutions in Africa, recognized for excellence, innovation, and sustainability, while setting industry standards for quality and customer satisfaction.

Strategic Goals

Key objectives driving our mission and vision forward

Market Leadership

Establish and maintain market leadership in refrigeration solutions across Africa

Innovation

Drive technological advancement and innovative solutions in the industry

Sustainability

Lead in environmentally sustainable refrigeration practices

Customer Success

Ensure customer success through excellence in service and support

Our Core Values

At De Koolar Nigeria Limited, our core values—Innovation, Excellence, Customer Satisfaction, Integrity, Passion, and Sustainability—are the foundation of our success and growth in the HVAC/R industry. These principles guide our decisions and shape our approach to every aspect of the business.

Innovation

Innovation is at the heart of what we do, driving us to find new and better ways to meet customer and industry needs through advanced technologies and continuous improvement.

Excellence

Excellence is a standard we uphold in product quality, customer service, and internal processes, aiming to exceed expectations and deliver long-lasting solutions.

Customer Satisfaction

Customer Satisfaction is our priority, ensuring we provide tailored solutions and build lasting relationships based on trust and exceptional service.

Integrity

Integrity is the cornerstone of our operations, emphasizing honesty, transparency, and ethical practices in all we do.

Passion

Passion fuels our work, motivating us to excel, innovate, and make a positive impact on our workplace and community.

Sustainability

Sustainability is central to our vision, ensuring we operate in ways that support environmental, social, and economic well-being, while balancing growth with responsibility.

These core values help us lead the HVAC/R industry and drive positive change, making De Koolar a trusted partner committed to creating lasting value for our customers, employees, and communities.
