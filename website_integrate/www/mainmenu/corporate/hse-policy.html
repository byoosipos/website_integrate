Our Vision

Commitment to Excellence in HSE

At De Koolar Nigeria Limited, we recognize that the health, safety, and well-being of our employees, contractors, customers, and the environment are of paramount importance. Our commitment to upholding the highest standards of Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) management is fundamental to the success and sustainability of our operations.

Our Foundation

Core Principles

Health and Well-being

We prioritize the health and safety of our workforce through comprehensive training and support systems.

02

Safety in Workplace

Safety is the cornerstone of our operations, with strict protocols and continuous monitoring.

03

Environmental Care

Committed to sustainable practices and minimizing environmental impact in all operations.

04

Compliance Standards

Adhering to and exceeding national and international HSE regulations and guidelines.

Our HSE Commitment

De Koolar is dedicated to ensuring that every aspect of our business operations is carried out in a safe and environmentally responsible manner. This commitment is embedded in our corporate culture and is reinforced through ongoing training, risk management, and a strict adherence to industry standards and regulations. Our commitment has also fetch us membership with OSHA, UK.

Key HSE Objectives

Eliminate Risks

Comprehensive risk assessments and mitigation measures across all operations.

Safety Culture

Building a safety-first culture with employee empowerment.

Environmental Care

Minimizing waste and promoting sustainable practices.

Continuous Growth

Regular reviews and updates of HSE systems and protocols.

HSE Management System

De Koolar has implemented an effective HSE management system (HSEMS) that covers all aspects of our operations, from design and manufacturing to installation and after-sales service. The system is based on internationally recognized standards and ensures that we:

Training & Implementation

  • Conduct regular health and safety training sessions
  • Implement emergency response plans
  • Monitor and review performance through audits

Safety & Equipment

  • Ensure equipment meets safety standards
  • Provide protective equipment and resources
  • Maintain comprehensive safety protocols

Employee Involvement and Responsibility

We believe that a successful HSE policy requires the active participation of all employees, from senior management to field workers. Our staff are encouraged to take personal responsibility for their own health and safety, as well as that of their colleagues.

Regular HSE Training

Comprehensive training sessions to ensure safety awareness and compliance.

Open Communication

Transparent dialogue about HSE concerns and improvements.

Active Participation

Employee engagement in safety measures and protocol development.

Incident Reporting and Investigation

De Koolar maintains a proactive approach to incident management with well-defined reporting and investigation processes. All incidents are promptly reported and thoroughly investigated to determine root causes and prevent recurrence.

Our Commitment to Excellence

De Koolar Nigeria Limited is fully committed to maintaining the highest standards of Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) management. Through continuous improvement, innovation, and a focus on safety, we remain a trusted leader in the HVAC/R industry.
