About DeKoolar

Nigeria's Leading Indigenous HVAC/R Solutions Provider

De Koolar Nigeria Limited stands as a proud, fully indigenous leader in the HVAC/R industry, with more than two decades of unparalleled expertise and a reputation built on trust and innovation. As the foremost leading Nigeria manufacturer and supplier, we offer a comprehensive array of advanced products, covering commercial refrigeration system, large cold storage warehouses, commercial and industrial blast freezers, cold/chiller rooms, including ice block making machine.

Our diverse product line is complemented by our specialized services in the design, installation, maintenance, and repair of HVAC/R systems tailored for domestic, commercial, and industrial applications.

At De Koolar, we are driven by a clear mission: to provide innovative and dependable heat transfer solutions that are perfectly aligned with the unique needs of our clients. We aim for nothing less than maximum efficiency, exceptional performance, and the highest level of customer satisfaction in every project we undertake.

Our History

A Legacy of Excellence in Heat Transfer Solutions

De Koolar Nigeria Limited's story began with humble roots as OLA AKANBI NIGERIA ENTERPRISES, a small refrigeration and air-conditioning business based in Abuja. With a relentless drive for progress and an eye toward the future, the company officially transformed into a limited liability company in January 2000. On February 7th of the same year, De Koolar Nigeria Limited was officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) under RC No. 373,575, marking the start of a transformative journey in the HVAC/R industry.

From its inception, De Koolar has been at the forefront of advancing heat transfer technologies, serving both domestic and industrial sectors with cutting-edge solutions. Our commitment to quality and excellence has propelled us to become a trusted name in the design, manufacture, servicing, and maintenance of state-of-the-art equipment. Over the years, we have especially earned a reputation as pioneers in the production of Ice Block Making Machines and Cold Rooms, areas in which we continue to excel and innovate.

As we look toward the future, De Koolar's unwavering dedication to technological advancement and customer-centric values continues to shape our path. Our core principles of passion, innovation, and relentless focus on customer satisfaction have positioned us as a true industry leader, with a growing list of satisfied clients across Nigeria.

When you choose De Koolar, you choose a legacy of excellence, integrity, and trust. Join us today and experience heat transfer solutions redefined for the modern age!
