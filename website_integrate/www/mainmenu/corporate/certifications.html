Our Certifications

Excellence Through Standards and Compliance

At De Koolar Nigeria Limited, we understand the importance of maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and performance in every product and service we provide. As a leader in the HVAC/R industry, we take pride in adhering to local and international standards, ensuring that our products and services meet or exceed the expectations of our customers.

Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) Registration

As a fully registered company, De Koolar Nigeria Limited is recognized by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) under RC No. 373,575. This certification validates our legal status as a limited liability company, ensuring that we operate in compliance with Nigerian business laws and regulations.

ISO Certification

De Koolar is ISO certified in relevant areas such as quality management (ISO 9001) and environmental management (ISO 14001) in view. These certifications are a testament to our commitment to international standards, focusing on consistent quality, customer satisfaction, and sustainability in our operations.

NAFDAC Compliance

Our Ice Block Making Machines and Cold Rooms are built to meet the rigorous health and safety standards required for the food and beverage industry. We ensure compliance with the regulations set forth by NAFDAC in production and ensuring that our products are safe, reliable, and fit for purpose.

Energy Efficiency Certifications

As part of our commitment to sustainability and energy conservation, De Koolar Nigeria Limited ensures that all of our air conditioning and refrigeration systems meet the highest energy efficiency standards. Our products are engineered to reduce energy consumption while maintaining optimal performance.

Manufacturer's Safety Certifications

All our equipment, including Blast Freezers, Industrial Ovens, and Display Chiller Fridges, undergo rigorous safety testing and are certified to meet national and international safety standards. These certifications guarantee that our products are safe for use in both domestic and industrial environments.

Technical and Industry Memberships

De Koolar Nigeria Limited is proud to be affiliated with prestigious organizations including:

  • Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE)
  • Nigeria Institute of Mechanical Engineer
  • Organization for Technology Advancement of Cold Chain in West Africa (OTACCWA)
  • Occupational Safety and Health Administrative UK

By maintaining these certifications, De Koolar Nigeria Limited reinforces its commitment to delivering cutting-edge, reliable, and high-quality HVAC/R solutions. We remain dedicated to continuous improvement and regulatory compliance, ensuring that our customers receive the very best products and services available in the market.
