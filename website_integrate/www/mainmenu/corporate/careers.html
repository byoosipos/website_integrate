Careers at De Koolar Nigeria Limited

Join Nigeria's Leading HVAC/R Solutions Provider

At De Koolar Nigeria Limited, we believe that our greatest asset is our people. We are a forward-thinking, innovative company in the HVAC/R industry, and our continued success is driven by the talent, passion, and dedication of our employees. As a company, we foster an inclusive and dynamic workplace where employees are encouraged to develop professionally and contribute meaningfully to the company's growth and success. We offer a range of exciting career opportunities for individuals who are ready to be part of an industry leader and are passionate about creating innovative solutions that make a real difference in society.

Why Work at De Koolar?

Innovation and Growth

De Koolar is at the forefront of the HVAC/R industry, and we pride ourselves on our ability to innovate and adapt to ever-changing market needs. When you join De Koolar, you will be part of a team that is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of technology and creativity.

Collaborative Culture

We foster a collaborative and inclusive work culture where employees feel empowered to share their ideas and contribute to the success of the company. Our team-oriented approach ensures that everyone has a voice and plays an integral role in shaping the future of De Koolar.

Professional Development

We offer various training programs, workshops, and mentoring opportunities designed to enhance your skills and expand your professional capabilities. From technical certifications in HVAC/R systems to leadership training, we are committed to your growth.

Competitive Benefits

We offer a competitive salary structure along with a comprehensive benefits package that includes health insurance, paid time off, retirement planning, and other perks that prioritize the well-being and security of our employees.

Diversity and Inclusion

We recognize the value of diversity in the workplace and are committed to providing equal employment opportunities to individuals from all backgrounds. We believe that diverse teams lead to more innovative ideas and better decision-making.

Career Opportunities at De Koolar

Engineering and Technical Roles

Our engineering and technical teams are the backbone of our business, responsible for the design, development, and maintenance of cutting-edge HVAC/R products. If you have expertise in refrigeration, air conditioning, or heat transfer systems, you could play a pivotal role.

Sales and Marketing

Join our dynamic sales and marketing team to help promote our products and services. Develop strategic marketing campaigns, nurture client relationships, and identify new market opportunities to drive De Koolar's continued success.

Customer Service and Support

Be part of our exceptional customer service team, ensuring clients receive the highest quality service. Focus on resolving issues, providing technical support, and helping clients maximize their De Koolar products.

Operations and Manufacturing

Join our operations team to ensure efficient delivery of high-quality products. From supply chain management and quality control to manufacturing and assembly, help maintain our high standards of excellence.

Human Resources and Administration

Support our organizational needs through talent acquisition, employee engagement, training, and fostering a positive company culture. Help create an environment where employees thrive.

Finance and Accounting

Join our finance team to manage budgets, financial reports, audits, and strategic planning. Make a significant impact on De Koolar's long-term financial success.

Research and Development (R&D)

Be part of our innovative R&D team researching emerging technologies and integrating them into our HVAC/R systems. Help shape the future of cooling solutions.

What We Look for in Candidates

  • Passion for Innovation
    A strong drive to contribute to technological advancement and new product development
  • Team-Oriented
    The ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues from diverse backgrounds and departments
  • Customer-Focused
    A commitment to understanding and meeting the needs of our clients
  • Problem-Solving Mindset
    A proactive approach to identifying challenges and finding creative solutions
  • Integrity
    A commitment to ethical practices and doing the right thing in all situations
